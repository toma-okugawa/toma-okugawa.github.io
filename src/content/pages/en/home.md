I'm a third-year student in the Information Science and Technology Department at NIT Yuge College, a KOSEN in Kamijima, Ehime, Japan. I research low-light image enhancement based on classical Retinex theory, and I have published the code and evaluation results of [SD-Retinex](/en/projects/sd-retinex/), a training-free method that is fast even on a CPU. Building on my research, I make [Kyudo AI](/en/projects/kyudo-ai/), an app that scores kyudo form on the phone, and [Beacon](/en/projects/beacon/), which clarifies inspection footage from dark places offline. I started programming in earnest with Python in 2025 and now also write Dart (Flutter) and TypeScript.
