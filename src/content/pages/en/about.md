I'm Toma Okugawa (奥河 董馬). I grew up on Yuge, a small island in Kamijima, Ehime, and I'm a third-year student in the Information Science and Technology Department at the National Institute of Technology, Yuge College (KOSEN).

I started programming in earnest in spring 2025. An interest in SQL brought me back to Python, and from May I built [YUMI-TRACK](/en/projects/yumi-track/), a web app that scores kyudo form. It led to a conference presentation and, later, the app [Kyudo AI](/en/projects/kyudo-ai/).

My research is low-light image enhancement based on classical Retinex theory. I released [FSSR](/en/projects/fssr/) and [XCR](/en/projects/xcr/) in 2025, and in 2026 [SD-Retinex](/en/projects/sd-retinex/), which fixes the problems of XCR. The research also powers [Beacon](/en/projects/beacon/), which works with inspection footage from dark places.

Since September 2026 I also make generative art ([t-okugawa.studio](https://t-okugawa.studio/en/)). I learn in public, failures included: the story so far is in my Medium essay “[Seeing in the Dark: How My Research Became Two Startups](https://medium.com/kairi-ai/seeing-in-the-dark-how-my-research-became-two-startups-2c8674f88334)” and in a [KOSEN MONSTER interview](https://kosen-monster.com/post/okugawa-toma) (in Japanese). The full list of my work is on [researchmap](https://researchmap.jp/t_okugawa).
