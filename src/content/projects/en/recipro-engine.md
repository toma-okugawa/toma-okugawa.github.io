The full title is “れしぷろえんじん！ — an engine that programs recipes”. A team of five entered it in the Original (free) Section of the KOSEN Programming Contest 2025.

The idea is that the process of cooking can be reproduced as an algorithm. By reproducing that mechanism as a web app, the project aims to teach home economics and programming at the same time.

We prepared for the final round from the first to the second semester of my second year, and received the Fighting Spirit Prize in the Original Section ([award record](https://www.procon.gr.jp/uploads/media/BaOh_qAwVAA)).
