A web app I built in Python alone between May and July 2025. A PC and a webcam film the archer, and MediaPipe pose estimation scores four measures: equal distance and the three crosses (*sanjū-jūmonji*: shoulders, hips and feet). Once it was finished, members of the Yuge College kyudo club tried it.

I presented this work as “Proposal and Web Implementation of a Kyudo Form Evaluation Method Based on Real-Time Pose Estimation” at the 2025 Shikoku-Section Joint Convention of the Institutes of Electrical and Related Engineers and received the [Outstanding Presentation Award](https://www.sjciee.org/%E5%84%AA%E7%A7%80%E7%99%BA%E8%A1%A8%E8%B3%9E-%E8%8B%B1%E8%AA%9E%E7%99%BA%E8%A1%A8%E5%A5%A8%E5%8A%B1%E8%B3%9E/%E4%BB%A4%E5%92%8C7%E5%B9%B4%E5%BA%A6%E5%8F%97%E8%B3%9E%E8%80%85/) ([publications](/en/publications/)).

A fixed PC and webcam are hard to bring into a dojo, and the app could not record hits. Rebuilding it as a smartphone app to solve this became [Kyudo AI](/en/projects/kyudo-ai/).
