Beacon works with inspection footage shot where light cannot reach, such as sewer pipes and ship tanks. It started as a business plan that joins low-light image enhancement research with AI.

- [SD-Retinex](/en/projects/sd-retinex/), training-free and CPU-only, corrects brightness and colour and brings out detail hidden in the shadows.
- Processing stays on the PC on site, with no internet connection, so footage never has to leave the organisation or go to the cloud.
- Detection and explanation with object detection and a local LLM are being built to run on site too.

The team is three people: Toma Okugawa, Uryu Den and Keito Inoshita.
